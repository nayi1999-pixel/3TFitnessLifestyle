MINDSET & TRAUMA: THE FOUNDATION OF 3T

At 3T Fitness, we understand that true transformation requires more than just picking up heavy weights. It requires a fundamental shift in how you view yourself, your body, and your past experiences.

Our methodology is deeply rooted in trauma-informed care. For many, the gym can be an intimidating, triggering, or unsafe space. Whether it's overcoming body dysmorphia, navigating the world as a trans individual, or dealing with past physical/emotional trauma, your lived experience matters.

We focus on nervous system regulation, establishing safety in your body, and using fitness as a tool for empowerment rather than punishment. You are in control of your journey here.

WHY DIETS FAIL: THE SUSTAINABLE LIFESTYLE

If you're tired of starting over every Monday, it's because you're relying on a system built to fail. Restrictive diets, cutting out entire food groups, and eating 1,200 calories a day are a fast track to burnout and binge eating.

At 3T, we teach sustainable nutrition. This means understanding your macronutrients, fueling your workouts properly, and yes—eating the foods you actually enjoy. Fitness should add to your life, not take away from it.

We build custom plans that fit your real life, whether you have 3 kids, work 60 hours a week, or travel constantly. Let's find your sustainable baseline.
